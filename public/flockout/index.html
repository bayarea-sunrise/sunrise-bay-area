GET FLOCK OUT OF SF

Flock Cameras Are Putting Us All at Risk

The Bay Area is home to thousands of Flock cameras, and our local billionaires spent millions to put them there.

What's at Stake?

Privacy

Flock cameras read your license plate to track your movements and store the data for long periods of time, creating a detailed record of your location history.

ICE Access

SFPD claims federal agencies like ICE don’t have access to Flock data, but they’ve been repeatedly caught not upholding this promise.

Officer Abuse

Data from Flock cameras has led to wrongful arrests, profiling, and stalking ex-partners by police officers.

Limited Benefits

There’s no substantial evidence that ALPRs effectively prevent crime, despite Flock’s unethical attempts to prove otherwise.
